 About Funded.Now 
 We organize prop firm pricing, rules, restrictions, platforms, and promotions so traders can compare the listed options and verify the details that matter.
 Who We Are
 We are a comparison and research hub for prop firm promotions, account pricing, evaluation rules, restrictions, platforms, and payout terms.
 Our mission is to make the available information easier to compare while being direct about affiliate relationships, data limitations, and details that still need to be checked with each firm.
 Whether you're a beginner exploring your first evaluation or an experienced trader scaling multiple accounts — this website was built for you.
