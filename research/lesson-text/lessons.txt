 USING THE LIBRARY
 Make the library your own.
 Read at your pace, save useful lessons, and come back to your notes.
 Do lessons have to be read in order? 
 No. Each lesson stands on its own, so choose any topic that interests you. Use the search and filters to find a lesson, then read at your own pace.
 How do bookmarks, read status, and notes save? 
 They save on this device and sync to your account when you’re signed in. A lesson becomes read when you choose “Mark as read”; opening it alone doesn’t change its read status.
 Can lessons replace a firm’s current rules? 
 No. Lessons are educational and use illustrative examples. They don’t promise performance or replace current firm rules, platform documentation, or your risk judgment. Use our firm comparison to explore listed terms, then confirm the current official requirements for your account.
