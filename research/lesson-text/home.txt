 Frequently Asked Questions 
 Quick answers to the most common questions about prop firms, challenges, and payouts.
 What is a prop trading firm? 
 A proprietary (prop) trading firm gives traders access to the firm's capital to trade futures, forex, or crypto. Traders typically pass an evaluation challenge to prove their skill, then trade a funded account and keep a share of the profits — commonly 80% to 90%.
 Which prop firm is best for beginners? 
 The best prop firm for beginners is one with an affordable challenge fee, a forgiving drawdown rule, no strict consistency requirement, and fast, reliable payouts. Funded.Now lets you filter firms by these exact criteria to find a beginner-friendly match.
 How much does a prop firm challenge cost? 
 Prop firm challenge fees usually range from about $30 to $600 depending on the account size and evaluation type. Many firms run discounts of 50% to 90% off, so the real 'true cost' of getting funded is often far lower than the listed price.
 How do prop firm payouts work? 
 Once funded, you withdraw a percentage of the profits you generate — typically an 80% to 90% profit split. Payout frequency varies by firm, from on-demand or daily payouts to fixed bi-weekly cycles. Funded.Now tracks each firm's profit split and payout speed.
 What is the 'true cost' of getting a funded account? 
 True cost is the total amount you actually pay to get funded — the challenge fee plus any activation or reset fee, minus the active discount code. Comparing true cost instead of the sticker price is the most accurate way to find the cheapest prop firm.
 Can I get a funded account without passing a challenge? 
 Yes. Some firms offer instant funding accounts that skip the evaluation entirely in exchange for a higher upfront fee. Funded.Now lets you filter for instant-funding and one-step evaluation firms.
