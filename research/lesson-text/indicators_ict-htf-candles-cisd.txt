 01 / Overview
 What this indicator does
 LumiTraders ICT HTF Candles + CISD is a free TradingView indicator that combines up to six higher-timeframe candle sets with sweep and CISD reclaim levels, multi-timeframe SMT, SMT on HTF candles, ICT projections, and retest, Early CISD and possible CISD/sweep labels on the chart.
 Use it when
 You want higher-timeframe candles beside the live chart, paired sweep and CISD reclaim levels, plus SMT and ICT projections.
 01 Six HTF candle sets Up to six sets of higher-timeframe candles are drawn to the right of price, each with its own timeframe and candle count, with day-of-week labels, candle time labels, remaining-time readout and a timezone selector. 
 02 Automatic timeframe pairing The chart timeframe selects its partner HTF automatically — 5m pairs to 1H, 15m to 4H, and so on, all editable — and the same pairing drives the drawn candles, the vertical boundary lines, the projected levels and the CISD model. 
 03 FVGs, imbalances and sweeps Fair Value Gaps and Volume Imbalances are marked on the HTF candles with an optional Consequent Encroachment line at each gap's 50%, and sweep lines mark a candle level a later candle traded through — optionally only true raids where the sweeping candle closed back inside. 
 04 Projected previous-candle levels The previous HTF candle's high, low and 50% midpoint are projected back onto the live chart and restyled once the forming HTF candle trades through them, scoped to the paired timeframe, the highest, the lowest, or all. 
 05 Close-confirmed CISD When an HTF candle sweeps the previous candle's low but closes back above it, the extreme open of the opposing run on the chart timeframe becomes the CISD level, drawn with its 50% once a candle closes back through it — the mirror case marks bearish setups. 
 06 Early detection and invalidation Early CISD watches the same reclaim on the chart timeframe while the HTF candle is still forming and is replaced by the confirmed line on close; a close through the 50% recolors a setup under pressure, and a close through the far side removes it — both measured on closes, never wicks. 
 07 Multi-timeframe SMT and ICT projections SMT includes a multi-timeframe option and support for HTF candles. ICT projections accompany the candle and CISD tools; the September 22 release further improves SMT. 
 08 Chart sweeps and setup labels Sweep lines extend to the lower chart timeframe. The latest release adds retest and Early CISD labels, plus labels for possible CISD and sweeps on that timeframe. 
 Early CISD is provisional while the higher-timeframe candle forms. A confirmed setup uses the completed candle; keep that distinction in view when reading the drawings.

 Settings, explained

 Setting names are paraphrased from the published description. Open the script settings on TradingView for the exact labels.
 Setting
 What it changes
 Pairing
 Pair timeframes
 The backbone. The chart timeframe is matched against this list and the result drives the drawn candles, vertical lines, projected levels and the CISD model. Off or unmatched, each of those falls back to its own timeframe setting.
 HTF candles
 Candle sets
 Up to six sets, each with its own timeframe and candle count. 50% lines of previous HTF candles can be drawn directly on a set for as many candles back as you want.
 Boundaries and traces
 Vertical lines at HTF candle boundaries, following the pairing or a fixed timeframe, and trace lines connecting each HTF candle's open, high, low and close back to where it happened on your chart.
 CISD
 Bias
 Restricts the model to long setups only, short only, or both.
 History
 How many past setups stay drawn. Default is one, which keeps the chart clean.
 CISD line length
 How far past the confirmation candle the lines are drawn.
 Max CISD age
 A level is only used if the candle that formed it is recent enough — guards against an old sweep anchoring a line thousands of bars back.
 Delete when invalidated
 On, invalidated lines are removed; off leaves them drawn until History pushes them off, if you want to review what failed.
 SMT
 Multi-timeframe SMT
 Adds an option to use SMT across multiple timeframes. The release notes do not specify its default timeframes or comparison symbols.
 04 / Reference
 Alerts and limitations
 Know what triggers an alert and which chart conditions affect the drawings.
 Before you use it
 Higher-timeframe data is requested with lookahead enabled; the model samples it at the HTF candle's close, so confirmed setups are evaluated against completed candles, but the request itself is not a lookahead-free one. 
 Early CISD is provisional by design — it draws on a level that has not been confirmed by an HTF close, and the forming bar can still change. 
 HTF candle sets and CISD lines share TradingView's 500-line and 500-box budget; with six timeframes, sweeps, levels and vertical lines all enabled, drawings can start disappearing. Reduce candle counts or enabled timeframes. 
 Levels and candle sets only draw on chart timeframes that divide evenly into the selected higher timeframe. 
 A few useful answers
 Frequently asked questions
 Is the ICT HTF Candles + CISD indicator free? 
 Yes. It is a free, protected-source script on TradingView. Sign in to a TradingView account to add it to your chart. TradingView's plan limits, market-data access and drawing limits still apply.
 How do I add it to my TradingView chart? 
 Open the script page on TradingView and click Add to favorites (you need to be signed in). Then choose Indicators › Favorites › LumiTraders ICT HTF Candles + CISD. There is no download; the indicator runs inside TradingView, including the mobile app.
 What changed in the September 22, 2026 update? 
 Early CISD was updated and SMT improved. The release adds retest and Early CISD labels, plus possible CISD and sweep labels on the lower chart timeframe, and credits MountainDude. Earlier September releases added multi-timeframe SMT, SMT on HTF candles and chart-timeframe sweep lines. The linked TradingView description and chart labels are in English.
 What is a CISD? 
 A Change In State of Delivery. The model looks for an HTF candle that takes out the previous candle's low but closes back above it (a swept low; the mirror for highs is the bearish case). The extreme open of the opposing run that built the sweep candle is the CISD level — the price delivery has to reclaim — and the setup is confirmed when a candle closes back through it. Two lines are drawn: the reclaim level and its 50%.
 How is it different from other HTF candle tools? 
 Most HTF candle tools stop at drawing the candles, and most CISD tools run on their own timeframe logic. Here the timeframe pairing that decides which candles are drawn is the same timeframe the CISD model is built from, so the levels on the chart always belong to the candles beside it.
 How is it meant to be used? 
 Set the pairing and let the chart timeframe pick the fractal: drop to 5m and you are reading 1H structure, drop to 1m and you are reading 15m. The HTF candles show where a sweep sits in context, the projected levels show what price is trading against, and the CISD lines mark where delivery changed. Once a setup is live, the 50% is the line to watch — a close through it is the first sign it is failing.
 Why did some drawings disappear? 
 The HTF candle sets and the CISD lines share TradingView's 500-line and 500-box budget. With six timeframes, sweeps, levels and vertical lines all enabled the budget can run out and drawings drop. Reduce the candle counts, the vertical line maximum, or the number of enabled timeframes.
 Is the source code open? 
 The script is published as protected source. It is built on ICT HTF Candles by fadi, used under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
 Does it generate buy or sell signals? 
 No. It is a tool for reading structure — HTF context, sweeps and reclaim levels. Nothing in it is a signal or trading advice.
